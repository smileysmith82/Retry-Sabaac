The ratings I gave are ultimately design judgments, not canon measurements. Here's the reasoning behind them.

What the three stats mean
Aggression
How willing they are to put more credits into the pot.

High aggression:

Raises more frequently.

Applies pressure.

Is less satisfied with checking/calling.

Is more likely to continue betting when they have a strong hand.

Low aggression:

Checks more.

Calls rather than raises.

Avoids unnecessarily growing the pot.

Risk
How willing they are to make a dangerous decision.

This is different from aggression.

Someone can be aggressive but relatively calculated.

For example, Thrawn might make a large raise with a strong hand, but he's unlikely to throw his entire stack away on a marginal hand.

Someone like Hondo might happily do both.

Bluff
How willing they are to represent strength when they don't actually have it.

High bluff:

Raises with mediocre/weak hands.

Calls bets that don't make much sense mathematically.

Occasionally makes intentionally deceptive plays.

Low bluff:

Mostly bets when they actually like their hand.

Doesn't try elaborate deception.

Is easier to read.

That distinction gives you some really interesting combinations.

Ahsoka
Aggression 0.55
Risk       0.45
Bluff      0.35

I see Ahsoka as balanced and disciplined.

She's willing to act, but she's not looking for unnecessary confrontation. She tends toward measured decisions rather than gambling.

So she's:

"I have a good hand, I'll bet. I don't need to prove anything."

That's why her aggression is slightly above average but her risk and bluff are lower.

Beckett
Aggression 0.80
Risk       0.75
Bluff      0.85

Beckett is one of the strongest fits for a Sabacc personality.

He's a professional gambler / con artist / opportunist.

His big trait is deception.

So:

High aggression → willing to pressure opponents.

High risk → willing to take chances.

Very high bluff → deception is central to his play.

He should be one of those AIs where the player thinks:

"I have no idea if Beckett actually has anything."

Bix is intended to be relatively cautious.

So she's more:

"Make the sensible play and survive."

This also works nicely because she's Easy difficulty. She's not necessarily bad at Sabacc; she's simply conservative.

The really important part
These numbers give you some distinct personality archetypes rather than just 30 slightly different AIs.

For example:

C-3PO	0.15	0.10	0.05	Extremely cautious
Thrawn	0.60	0.25	0.70	Calculating
Chewbacca	0.65	0.80	0.20	Aggressive risk-taker
Lando	0.75	0.70	0.90	Charismatic gambler
Han	0.85	0.85	0.90	Reckless gambler
Palpatine	0.85	0.60	0.85	Manipulator
Hondo	0.90	0.95	0.95	Chaotic gambler
Saw	0.95	0.95	0.45	Relentless
Vader	0.90	0.75	0.35	Intimidating

And that's exactly what I want the personality system to accomplish.

The next step I'd recommend is not adding more personality stats yet. We should take these three and figure out exactly how aggression, risk, and bluff modify your existing make_betting_move() without turning it into a giant mess of if statements.
